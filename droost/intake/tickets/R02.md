# R02 — Vehicles: the inventory, its filters and details, and the home page's picks

**Builds:** the Vehicle content type (title worked out from year, make and model; stock number, make,
model, year, price, body style, mileage, color, condition, featured, sale status, photo); the Make
vocabulary; the Body style vocabulary (name, plural name, tile line, tile photo, order) seeded with Truck,
SUV and Car; the 15 sample vehicles with their photos, all Available; the vehicle card; the page heading; the Inventory View page at
/vehicles with its filters and the vehicle details pop-up; the redirect from a vehicle's own address;
the home page's featured vehicles, body-style tiles and live vehicle count; the /vehicles.html redirect.

**Consumes:** R01 (the theme, the frame, the dealership details' phone for the pop-up's Call button, the
home page).

**Pages (parity):** /vehicles, /

**Source:** `vehicles.html`, `src/site/vehicles.ts`, `src/site/home.ts`, `carCard()` and `VEHICLES` in
`src/site/shared.ts`, `public/images/v1.jpg`–`v15.jpg`.

## Acceptance criteria

- /vehicles shows "Sample inventory · not live stock", "The lot." and its lede, then all 15 vehicles as
  cards in the source's default order (featured first, then by stock number), with "15 vehicles of 15".
- Each card shows its photo, one chip ("Sale pending" if it is, else "Featured" if it is, else its body
  style), "2021 · TRUCK", "Ford F-150",
  "42,000 mi" and "Magnetic Gray", "$38,900" with "Sample price", and Details.
- Search matches year, make, model, colour or body style; body style (All, then a button per body style
  with a car on the lot, by its plural name, in staff's order: Trucks, SUVs, Cars to start), make (All
  makes and the makes of the listed cars: the seven to start), maximum price, minimum year and maximum mileage narrow the list together,
  and the count, the sliders' read-outs and the active-filter chips follow at once.
- Each chip removes its filter; with two or more, "Clear all" resets them; Reset does the same.
- The five sorts order the list as the source's do (featured first, price up, price down, newest first,
  lowest mileage).
- A combination with no match shows "Nothing matches that combination." with "Reset all filters" and
  "Ask us to look" (to /contact with the topic Vehicle search).
- At 390 wide the filters hide behind a "Filters" button that toggles to "Hide filters".
- /vehicles?type=Truck opens with Trucks chosen; an unknown type opens with All.
- With the 15 samples the sliders run $25k–$60k, 2019–2022 and 10k–80k, as the source's do; a vehicle an
  editor adds outside those bounds widens them, so it is listed by default.
- Details opens the pop-up: "TRUCK · STOCK #001", "2021 Ford F-150", "$38,900" with "Sample listed
  price", Mileage, Year, Body style, Color, Make and Condition, the sample note, "Ask about this car"
  (to /contact?vehicle=1) and "Call (sample)". The address gains `?vehicle=1`; Escape, the close button
  or a click outside closes it, drops the parameter and returns focus to its Details button.
- /vehicles?vehicle=3 opens with the RAM 1500's pop-up showing.
- A vehicle's own node address redirects to /vehicles?vehicle=<its stock number> (Q5).
- / shows "Featured this week" with the featured vehicles that are not sold as cards (the four to start),
  each Details opening /vehicles?vehicle=<stock number>, and "All vehicles"; then "What do you need it to
  do?" with a tile per body style with a car on the lot, three to a row: Trucks ("Haul, tow, work"), SUVs
  ("Room for everyone") and Cars ("Just for driving") to start, with the source's photos, linking to
  /vehicles?type=Truck, SUV and Car.
- The hero's tag reads "15 sample vehicles" and counts the listed vehicles (available and sale pending).
- Staff mark each car Available, Sale pending or Sold. A sold car leaves /vehicles, the home page's
  featured cars and count, and the filters' choices; a link to it (/vehicles?vehicle=<its stock number>)
  opens the inventory without a pop-up (Q11).
- A sale-pending car stays listed with a "Sale pending" chip in place of its Featured or body-style chip,
  and the chip in its pop-up; it can still be asked about (Q10).
- Staff add a body style (say Van, "Vans", its tile line and photo) and set the order; once a car of
  that style is on the lot, its button joins the filter and its tile joins the home page, a fourth tile
  starting a second row; /vehicles?type=Van opens with it chosen (Q12).
- An editor adds a vehicle by typing its stock number, make, model, year, price, mileage, colour and
  condition, choosing its body style and photo; its title is written for them. Marking it featured puts it
  on the home page; unpublishing it takes it off every page.
- The make is chosen from the Make vocabulary, so each make appears once in the filter.
- /vehicles.html, /vehicles.html?type=Truck and /vehicles.html?vehicle=3 redirect to /vehicles with the
  same query.
