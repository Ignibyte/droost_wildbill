# R01 — The look, the dealership details, the frame and the home page's copy

**Builds:** the Wild Bill's theme and its tokens; the site's identity; the dealership details settings
(name, phone, email, street, city, weekly hours, time zone, demo notices); the main and footer menus;
the site header and footer; the home page's hero, promise band, how-it-works, questions-to-ask and
closing call-to-action sections on the existing Home Canvas page; the /index.html redirect.

**Consumes:** nothing (the first rung).

**Pages (parity):** frame, /

**Source:** `design/src/Wild-Bills-Cars/artifacts/wildbills` — `index.html`, `src/site/shared.ts`
(`DEALER`, `HOURS`, `NAV`, `initShell`), `src/site/site.css`. The approved direction is the forest-green
and cream design; the handoff forbids the rejected western look (no western fonts, wood, emoji or
"pardner" copy).

## Acceptance criteria

- Every page shows the source's header: the demo bar ("Demo site. Inventory, phone, email, address and
  hours shown here are sample information, not a live dealership listing."), the WB mark with "Wild
  Bill's / Used Cars" linking home, the links Home, Inventory, Contact and Visit with the current page
  marked, and a "Browse cars" button to /vehicles.
- At 390 wide the links fold into a menu button; opening it shows the four links and "Call (555)
  123-4567 (sample)"; Escape closes it and returns focus to the button.
- Every page shows the source's footer: the brand and "An independent used-car lot. Look at the car, ask
  your questions, decide in your own time."; Pages (Home, Inventory, Contact, Location & hours); Hours
  (sample) as "Mon–Fri 8am–7pm", "Saturday 9am–6pm", "Sunday closed"; Contact (sample) with the phone
  and email as links and the two-line address; "© <this year> Wild Bill's Used Cars · Demonstration
  website" and "Vehicle photos are illustrative. Contact details are placeholders."
- The footer shows no Privacy policy and no My privacy settings link, and no consent dialog appears
  (Q7).
- A "Skip to content" link is the first thing a keyboard reaches; focus is visible on every control.
- The header and footer match the parity reference at 1280 and 390 wide, in the source's three faces.
- An editor changes the phone, email, address, any day's hours or the time zone in one settings form,
  and the header, footer and every page that shows it follow; the footer's hours summary is worked out
  from the week's hours.
- An editor turns the demo notices off in that form, and the demo bar, every "(sample)" and "sample"
  label and the footer's demonstration lines disappear; at launch they are on (Q2).
- / shows, in order: the hero ("Good used cars. Plainly sold." with "Plainly" in italics, the lede,
  "Browse inventory" and "Ask a question", the lot photo with its "15 sample vehicles" tag and "See
  them"); the four promise pairs; "Four steps. No runaround." with its four numbered steps; "Bring your
  questions. We'd rather you ask." with the office photo, four checklist lines, "Send a question" and
  "Hours & directions"; and "Found one you like?" with "Browse inventory" and "Plan a visit". The
  featured vehicles and body-style tiles come in R02.
- An editor changes any of the home page's copy in Canvas, one component per section (HR2).
- The site's name is "Wild Bill's Used Cars"; / is titled "Wild Bill's Used Cars — Straightforward used
  cars, trucks and SUVs" with the source's description; the favicon is the source's green one.
- Sections that reveal on scroll appear without motion when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
- /index.html answers with a redirect to /.
