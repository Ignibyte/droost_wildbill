# R04 — Contact: the inquiry form

**Builds:** the Contact Canvas page at /contact; the inquiry form that drafts an email in the visitor's
own mail app, prefilled from a vehicle or a topic; the four contact cards; the /contact.html redirect.

**Consumes:** R02 (the vehicles, by stock number, for the prefilled inquiry), and through it R01 (the
dealership details' email, phone, address and hours). It reuses R03's info card, since the rungs run in
order.

**Pages (parity):** /contact

**Source:** `contact.html`, `src/site/contact.ts`.

## Acceptance criteria

- /contact shows "Contact", "Ask us anything." and its lede.
- The form shows the notice "This site has no message server. Submitting prepares an email in your own
  mail app. Nothing is sent until you press send there." and the fields Your name, Email, Phone
  (optional), Topic (General question, Specific vehicle, Test drive, Trade-in, Payment options, Vehicle
  search) and Message with its "0 / 1000" count, which follows as the visitor types.
- Submitting with a field missing shows the source's messages ("Please add your name.", "Please add your
  email." or "That email address doesn't look complete.", "Please write a short message.") and moves
  focus to the first; nothing is posted to the site (Q4).
- A valid form is replaced by "Not sent yet. Your message is ready but has not left this page." and
  "Your email is ready to send.", with the To, Subject and body preview, "Open in email app" (a mailto
  to the dealership's email with that subject and body), "Copy message" (with its copied or select-it
  status) and "Edit message", which brings the form back as it was.
- /contact?vehicle=1 shows the "ASKING ABOUT" card for the 2021 Ford F-150 with its price, miles and
  colour and a Remove button, sets the topic to Specific vehicle and fills the source's message; the
  draft's subject is "Inquiry: 2021 Ford F-150" and its body carries the stock number, price and the
  listing's address (/vehicles?vehicle=1). Remove drops the vehicle and the parameter.
- /contact?topic=Test%20drive opens with Test drive chosen; an unknown topic, or the stock number of a
  sold or unlisted car, is ignored (Q11); a sale-pending car can still be asked about (Q10).
- The side cards show Phone, Email, Visit (with "Hours & directions" to /location) and Hours, each from
  the dealership details, each with its "sample" tag while the demo notices are on.
- The page is titled "Contact — Wild Bill's Used Cars" with the source's description.
- /contact.html, /contact.html?vehicle=3 and /contact.html?topic=Trade-in redirect to /contact with the
  same query.
