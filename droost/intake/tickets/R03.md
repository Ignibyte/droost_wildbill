# R03 — Location & hours

**Builds:** the Location & hours Canvas page at /location; the hours table with today marked and the
open status in the dealership's time zone; the map card with its Google and Apple Maps links; the info
card; the /location.html redirect.

**Consumes:** R01 (the theme, the frame, the dealership details' address, hours and time zone, the
closing call-to-action component).

**Pages (parity):** /location

**Source:** `location.html`, `src/site/location.ts`, `openStatus()`, `fmtHour()` and `HOURS` in
`src/site/shared.ts`.

## Acceptance criteria

- /location shows "Visit", "Come see it in person." and its lede.
- The map card shows the illustrative map with its "Wild Bill's" pin and the caption "Illustrative
  graphic, not a real map. The address below is a sample placeholder." (Q6); "Open in Google Maps" and
  "Apple Maps" open the address in those services in a new tab.
- "Address" with its "sample" tag shows the street and city from the dealership details; "Before you
  head over" shows its advice and "Ask about a test drive" (to /contact with the topic Test drive).
- "Hours" lists Monday to Sunday from the week's hours ("8:00am – 7:00pm", "9:00am – 6:00pm", "Closed"),
  with today marked "· today".
- The status reads "Open now · closes 7:00pm", "Closed · opens today at 8:00am", "Closed · opens tomorrow
  at …" or "Closed · opens <day> at …", reckoned in the dealership's time zone, not the visitor's clock,
  and stays right on a cached page (Q8).
- The note under the hours reads "Status uses the dealership's local time." (Q8, recorded in
  droost/parity/decided.json).
- "Shortlist first." with "Browse inventory" sits under the hours.
- An editor changes the page's copy in Canvas; the address and hours come only from the dealership
  details.
- The page is titled "Location & Hours — Wild Bill's Used Cars" with the source's description.
- /location.html redirects to /location.
